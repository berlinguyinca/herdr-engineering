## N2: throttle can drop the final snapshot
The 30 s per-mission throttle (and now the unchanged-skip) decide on the stored mirror, not on the latest snapshot. A change that lands inside the 30 s window after a write is not mirrored until a later snapshot arrives for that mission. If the control plane restarts in that gap, the mirror holds the older state (at most 30 s of change; an unchanged mission at most 10 min old by timestamp, still within the 24 h retention). In-memory state is always current. Not changed on purpose; the connection-state flip is exempt.

## Rollback
- Code: revert the G10 commits (no schema change). Flag: leave `work_center` off and the mirror stops writing, pruning and hydrating after the restart.
- Leftover mirror rows after a rollback are never served (private type) and expire by retention only while the mirror runs; remove them with:
  `DELETE FROM fabric_events WHERE entity_type='mission_activity_store'`
- Deploy order: Herdr first, then Pi (S5).
- Checkpoints: `code_fingerprint` hashes every control_plane `*.py`, so this build publishes a new checkpoint; a checkpoint written by an earlier build is not selected unless the compatibility manifest lists it.
